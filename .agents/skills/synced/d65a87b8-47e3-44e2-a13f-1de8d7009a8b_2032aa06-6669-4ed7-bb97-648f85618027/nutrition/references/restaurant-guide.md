# Restaurant and On-the-Go Guide
## Ordering On-Plan at Common Restaurant Types

---

## The Universal Order Strategy

At any restaurant, apply this in order:
1. **Find the protein anchor first** — what's the highest-protein, lowest-carb entrée?
2. **Sub the starch** — replace fries, rice, or bread with a side salad, steamed veg, or double veg
3. **Sauce and dressing on the side** — sauces are where hidden calories and sugar live
4. **Ask, don't assume** — how is it cooked? Grilled vs. fried changes the equation significantly

---

## By Restaurant Type

### American / Bar & Grill
**Order:**
- Grilled chicken, steak, or salmon — no sauce or sauce on side
- Double vegetable side in place of fries or potato
- Side salad with oil and vinegar (not creamy dressing)

**Skip:**
- Burgers (the bun kills it; lettuce wrap if available)
- Anything fried
- Loaded sides (mac and cheese, mashed potatoes with butter)

**Hack:** Ask for a burger as a lettuce wrap or protein plate — many kitchens will do this.

---

### Mexican
**Order:**
- Bowl (not burrito) — protein + salsa + guacamole, no rice, no tortilla
- Fajita meat on a plate with veg, skip the tortillas
- Carnitas or grilled chicken over ground beef (slightly leaner)

**Skip:**
- Chips (even a small basket is 300+ calories before the meal starts)
- Sour cream (add calories without protein)
- Rice and beans combo (high carb, moderate protein — not worth it on a cut)

**Macro estimate for a good bowl (protein + guac + salsa, no rice, no tortilla):** ~35g protein, 8–12g carbs, ~420 cal

---

### Asian (Chinese, Thai, Japanese)
**Chinese/Thai — difficult to navigate, high hidden sugar in sauces:**
- Steamed protein + steamed vegetables, sauce on side
- Hot and sour soup or egg drop soup (low calorie, surprisingly filling)
- Skip fried rice, noodles, and anything in a "sauce" (often sugar-heavy)

**Japanese — one of the better options:**
- Sashimi (fish only, no rice) — very high protein, minimal calories
- Edamame as a starter (high protein, reasonable carbs)
- Miso soup (low calorie, some protein)
- Skip sushi rolls (rice + often cream cheese/mayo — carb-heavy)

**Korean:**
- Grilled meats (bulgogi, galbi) are often well-seasoned and protein-dense
- Skip the rice; eat the banchan (side dishes) instead
- Bibimbap without rice if possible

---

### Italian
**Hardest category for low-carb — plan ahead or eat before.**
- Grilled fish or chicken is almost always an option as a secondi (main)
- Caprese salad as a starter (protein from mozzarella + good fat from olive oil)
- Zuppa di pesce (seafood stew) — high protein, no pasta

**Skip:**
- Pasta (the entire purpose of the category)
- Bread basket (eat zero — the willpower cost is low if you decide before sitting down)
- Risotto

---

### Fast Food (When There's No Better Option)
- **Chipotle:** Bowl, double protein, fajita veg, salsa, skip rice/beans/tortilla → ~40g protein, 500 cal
- **Chick-fil-A:** Grilled chicken sandwich (discard bun), grilled nuggets, side salad with vinaigrette
- **McDonald's:** Grilled chicken snack wrap without the wrap (ask for a side), side salad
- **Subway:** Protein bowl (no bread) — double meat, extra veggies, mustard/vinegar
- **In-N-Out:** Protein-style burger (lettuce wrap), no fries

## Coffee Shops

**Order:**
- Black coffee or espresso
- Unsweetened iced coffee or cold brew
- If adding milk: unsweetened almond milk or a splash of whole milk (not oat milk — high carb)

**Skip:**
- Any flavored latte (syrups = 20g+ sugar per pump, 2–3 pumps per drink)
- Frappuccinos and blended drinks (300–600 calories, mostly sugar)
- "Protein boxes" — often include crackers and cheese at higher calorie cost than they look

## Social Eating (The Real Challenge)

**The pre-eat strategy:** Eat a protein-rich snack (Greek yogurt, hard-boiled eggs, protein shake) before going to a social event. Arrive not hungry. This removes desperation decisions.

**The buffet strategy:** Make one pass. Fill the plate with protein and vegetables first. Sit down before going back. One pass usually satisfies.

**The alcohol strategy:** If drinking, choose dry wine or spirits with soda water over beer or sugary cocktails. Alcohol stalls fat oxidation for hours after consumption — not a reason to never drink, but a reason to keep it minimal during a cut.

**What to say when people comment on your eating:** "I'm just not that hungry tonight" ends 90% of conversations. You don't owe anyone an explanation.
