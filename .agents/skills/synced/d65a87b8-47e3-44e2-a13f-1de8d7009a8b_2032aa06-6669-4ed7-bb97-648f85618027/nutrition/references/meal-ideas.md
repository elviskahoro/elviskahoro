## Batch Prep Anchors (Cook Once, Eat Multiple Days)

**Proteins to batch:**
- Bake 4–6 chicken breasts at once (375°F, 25–30 min). Season minimally — versatile for multiple meals.
- Brown 2 lbs ground beef or turkey. Portion into 4 oz servings. Store 4 days.
- Hard-boil a dozen eggs. Peel and refrigerate. Ready for 5 days.

**Vegetables to batch:**
- Sheet pan roast: any combination of broccoli, cauliflower, zucchini, asparagus, bell peppers. 400°F, 20 min. Stores 4 days.

**Assembly rule:** With batch-prepped protein and veg in the fridge, any meal takes under 5 minutes to assemble. Remove friction = stay on plan.

## When Calories Are Very Low (Aggressive Cut)

Prioritize volume and protein above all:
1. Egg whites over whole eggs
2. Shrimp or tuna over beef or chicken thigh (more protein per calorie)
3. Unlimited non-starchy vegetables to fill the plate
4. Broth-based soups (low-sodium if retaining water)
5. Black coffee to manage hunger without breaking fast
6. Sparkling water with electrolytes to manage salt cravings

Do not use nuts, nut butters, oils, or full-fat dairy on very low calorie days — calorie density is too high.
